Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes our data handling standards for BallBlitz Frenzy, a ball themed adventure puzzle mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Service"). By downloading, installing, accessing or using this Service, you confirm that you have read, understood and accept all data processing rules defined in this document. Unless otherwise specified, all terms used in this policy adopt the same interpretation from our Terms of Service. We collect personal information only for operating, refining and securing in app features. We will not utilise or pass on your personal data to outside parties except under conditions clearly stated within this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We stick to data minimisation principles and only gather information required to deliver stable ball adventure gameplay, maintain account functions and elevate overall player experience. Collected data categories are listed below:

•                      Account and Profile Information: If you sign in with authorised third party platforms including Apple ID or Google, we obtain your verified email address, public display name and profile photo. This dataset is used for identity validation, creating your unique game profile, and syncing saved adventure progress, unlocked ball skins and collected in game treasures across multiple devices. We will not obtain extra private content unrelated to account setup.

•                      Advertising Identifier (IDFA / GAID): We gather device specific advertising identifiers for anti risk verification. This helps us spot abnormal behaviours including fake registrations, repeated reward claiming and abusive ad interactions. These identifiers also support our ad partners to deliver suitable non intrusive advertisements inside the application.

•                      IP Address: Your public IP address is collected to judge approximate regional access range, diagnose unstable server connections, resolve ad loading failures and block suspicious login attempts. We will not use IP data to pinpoint your precise physical location.

•                      Device and System Metadata: This covers app session start end timestamps, system language preferences, device hardware model, screen display parameters, time zone configuration and operating system version. Such metadata allows us to optimise puzzle scene rendering for various hardware models and fix device specific compatibility defects.

•                      Game Usage and Diagnostic Logs: We collect anonymous gameplay records including adventure level attempt frequency, stage passing speed, hint item consumption, difficulty preference settings and interactions with bonus tasks and advertisements. Crash and runtime error logs are also gathered. These records help our development team monitor service status, reproduce technical faults and continuously improve game content.

•                      Installed Application List (User Consent Required): Our integrated advertising SDK may send out a permission request to read your local application catalogue on initial launch. You hold full right to reject this prompt. Denying this permission will have no impact on core ball adventure puzzle functions. Our development team cannot view or store this list; all related processing is completed independently by the corresponding ad service provider following its own privacy provisions.

2. How We Make Use Of Your Information

Every set of collected data shall only be processed for legitimate service related purposes listed below. We will never repurpose your information for unapproved commercial activities without your separate explicit consent.

•                      Operate and sustain the Service: Your registered email is used for account authentication, tracking ball adventure progress, recording treasure collection status and managing reward archives. When reward settlement functions are activated, relevant account markers will be processed to guarantee correct reward distribution. Advertising identifiers are used according to ad partner requirements to display advertisements and unlock in app bonus tasks.

•                      User communication scenarios: We will send emails to your registered mailbox in two main situations. First, to receive and confirm your account deletion request and inform you of processing progress. Second, to deliver official notices for major revisions of this Privacy Policy or Terms of Service, so you can understand adjustments that may affect your user rights.

•                      Customer support services: When you submit feedback about game mechanics, reward exceptions, progress loss or other technical failures, your email serves as our communication channel. We may reference anonymous runtime logs to locate fault sources and speed up problem solving.

3. Information Disclosure Circumstances

We place high priority on user privacy protection. Your personal identifiable data will not be sold, leased or traded for commercial promotion. Information may only be shared under these limited compliant conditions:

•                      Sharing with authorised ad partners: Your advertising identifier can be transmitted to trusted ad service providers to complete ad rendering and bonus task delivery. All cooperating vendors must sign binding confidentiality agreements. They can only use this identifier for assigned ad related work and need to obey local data protection regulations.

•                      Corporate business transition events: If business merger, asset acquisition, company reorganisation or bankruptcy occurs, user information may transfer as part of business assets to the successor organisation. The receiving organisation must follow the protection requirements of this Privacy Policy. We will notify users by in app popup or email when local laws demand such notification.

•                      Legal mandatory requirements: We may disclose necessary user information responding to formal court orders, regulatory investigation notices or law enforcement inquiries. Under such circumstances, we only provide the minimal dataset required to fulfil legal obligations and will notify you unless forbidden by law.

4. Data Security Protection Measures

We deploy multiple technical and administrative safeguards to defend your personal information against unauthorised access, tampering, leakage and accidental loss.

•                      All data transmitted between your mobile terminal and backend servers adopts TLS 1.3 encryption. Account related archives saved on servers are protected by AES 256 static encryption technology.

•                      Strict access permission control is implemented. Only staff and contracted collaborators with real business needs can access non public user data. All relevant personnel have signed formal confidentiality commitments.

•                      We carry out regular system security audits, vulnerability scanning and software patch updates to cope with continuously emerging network security risks.

Please keep in mind that no internet transmission or electronic storage method can achieve complete absolute security. We adopt all commercially feasible protective means but cannot fully eliminate potential data safety risks.

5. Data Retention Rules

We keep personal information only within the time frame required to fulfil collection purposes or satisfy statutory compliance obligations. Once data is no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymise such content.

•                      After your account deletion application passes review, most personal data including adventure progress, usage logs and profile materials will be erased right away.

•                      For anti fraud needs such as preventing repeated reward redemption described in Terms of Service, partial non identifiable payment related markers may be reserved for up to six months. Name, email and avatar will still be removed immediately upon deletion approval.

•                      When local laws require extended preservation of certain records, we will anonymise user data as much as possible during the retention cycle.

6. Procedures For Submitting Data Deletion Request

If you hope to permanently erase your BallBlitz Frenzy account together with all bound game related data, you may choose one of two submission approaches.

  1.                 In App Self service Operation: Open BallBlitz Frenzy, go to Settings page and enter Account and Privacy section. Submit deletion application following on screen guidance. Identity verification through your registered email may be required.
  1.                 Email Application: Send your request email with subject: BallBlitz Frenzy Full Account and Data Deletion Request. Please attach your registered login email address for identity checking. Our team will complete identity verification within 3 business days and finish deletion work within 14 business days, except situations limited by legal or technical factors. Confirmation mail will be sent after processing finishes.

Please note: Once deletion completes, all adventure progress, collected treasures, unlocked skins and account content will disappear permanently and cannot be restored.

7. Runtime Log Data

When the app meets runtime failures such as crashing, screen freezing or scene loading errors, third party diagnostic tools will automatically collect anonymous log data. Log content may contain public IP address, device model, operating system version, app running parameters when error occurs, error occurrence timestamp and fault related statistical indicators. These logs are only used for troubleshooting and performance optimisation. Log data does not carry personal identifiers and will not be applied for other usage scenarios.

8. Cookies and Comparable Tracking Technologies

BallBlitz Frenzy itself will not deploy first party cookies for core puzzle gameplay. However integrated third party SDKs from ad or analytics partners may use cookies or local storage to support their service functions. You are able to adjust cookie acceptance options inside your device privacy settings. Rejecting third party cookies may affect personalised ad effects but will not block core ball adventure playing experience.

With your explicit permission, we can request access to your device contact list. This permission is used for anti fraud judgement and potential friend invite gameplay modules. Contact information will be removed from our server within 3 business days if you revoke authorisation. We will never forward contact data to third parties without your additional consent.

We will periodically check their privacy execution status.
